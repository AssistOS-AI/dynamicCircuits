Eval 3: cross-document consistency

This case checks whether repeated document interpretation preserves source identity and whether synthesis reports every conflicting launch date and definition without inventing a precedence rule.

How the evaluation works

The coding agent receives three prose chapters, one review-policy document, and one task document. The generated interpretation.sop extracts dates and the definition of “Node” from one chapter while retaining its source label. root.sop creates three independent child instances and sends their outputs to consistency.sop. The final goal requires both expected conflict classes.

Unlike eval 1 and eval 2, this runtime case still supplies chapter text through circuit inputs because the reusable interpretation circuit is the behavior under test. The inputs are source text, not JSON. The result keeps both alternatives and their source labels because the documents contain no supersession rule.
