# Vignette brief

Title: The Brass Observatory

Protagonist: Mara Ionescu

Setting: an abandoned hilltop observatory during the first winter storm

Concrete object: a brass compass that points toward remembered places

Immediate goal: recover her brother's final notebook before the roof collapses

Obstacle: the frozen dome mechanism will open only if she releases the compass into its gears

Choice: Mara sacrifices the compass and turns the dome by hand

Consequence: the notebook is saved, but every route home becomes unfamiliar

Closing image: snow settling inside the open dome like a new map

Motifs, in assigned order: red thread; clockwork breath; unmarked north
